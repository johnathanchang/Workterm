# Research Notes (Oct 30, 2026)

How we got to Workterm.

## The winning formula

Two apps built by friends prove students pay for this pattern:

**Your profile in → matched opportunities + AI-written applications out.**

| | Prof Insider | Award Scholar |
|---|---|---|
| Input | Resume | Profile + writing samples |
| Database ChatGPT doesn't have | Verified professors | Scholarships |
| AI's job | Custom email per professor | Custom essay per scholarship |
| Outcome | Research position | Money |

Prof Insider claims 18,000+ research positions landed, 100,000+ students and 2.4M+ emails sent. It sends from the student's own Gmail with follow-ups.

Why these work: tied to money or grades, they own a list ChatGPT doesn't have, and they hit a high-stakes moment.

## Markets we looked at

| Market | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Internships via alumni and recruiters | Crowded | Offerloop, Whali, NextCoffee, JobMail AI, Warm Intro (free) |
| Athlete recruiting | Crowded | NCSA ($1,320 to $4,000+), ProductiveRecruit, NextCommit.AI |
| Scholarships in Canada | Crowded | GrantMe, ScholarshipsCanada AI search |
| Pre-health shadowing | Open, but lower pain | Shadowing is unpaid and many students find spots on their own |
| Pre-med letters of recommendation | Open, add-on only | Profs overloaded; low frequency |
| Pre-med secondary essays | Crowded + ethics risk | Premed Copilot, MedSchool Copilot, GradPilot; AI-written essays break AMCAS certification |
| Business (coffee chats, case prep) | Saturated | Offerloop; PrepBuddy, CaseGrade, CaseStudyPrep, Kasie, AlumnAI, Case Cruncher |
| **Engineering co-ops** | **Chosen** | Required to graduate, job boards short on jobs, small local firms often don't post |

## Why engineering co-ops over shadowing

- Shadowing is easier to get and unpaid, so there's less pressure and lower willingness to pay.
- Co-ops are much harder, pay real wages, and are sometimes required to graduate.
- Both use the same engine (find local places that don't post openings, write a personal email, track replies), so shadowing stays as a second market.
