# Workterm – Product Brief

_Last updated: Oct 30, 2026. Status: niche chosen, validation not started._

## One-liner

For **engineering co-op students**, **Workterm** turns **your resume** into **personalized emails to local firms that don't post jobs** in **about 2 minutes**, then tells you what to work on to get hired.

## Who it's for

Canadian engineering co-op students who can't find a placement on their school's job board.

Why them:
- Some programs require co-ops to graduate (some Waterloo programs require 5 to 6).
- School job boards don't have enough jobs. Students report 100 to 235+ applications with little or no response.
- A co-op pays real wages, so paying to land one is an easy yes.

## Core loop

1. **Set up** – resume upload, school, program, co-op term, city.
2. **Find firms** – local small and mid-sized engineering firms that don't post co-op jobs. Match score plus a one-line reason.
3. **Send emails** – AI writes one personal email per firm using the firm's real projects and the student's real experience. Sends from the student's own Gmail, paced, with follow-ups.
4. **Track replies** – one inbox view. AI drafts responses.
5. **What to work on** – compares the resume to what target firms do and suggests a skill or weekend project. Updates as targets change, which gives a weekly reason to come back.

**Later:**

## Pricing

- **Free:** see firm matches, send 5 emails.
- **Paid:** one price per co-op term (around $30 to $40), unlimited emails and follow-ups.

## Unit economics (rough, to be checked after validation)

Using the playbook's default rates: 30% start a trial × 30% trial to paid × $35 per term ≈ **$3.15 per download**. Students search 4 to 6 terms per degree, so a returning student is worth more than one term. These are guesses until we have real numbers.

## Risks and counters

| Risk | Counter |
|---|---|
| Looks like spam | Send from the student's own Gmail, daily caps, every email unique, one contact per firm at a time |
| Low reply rates | Set expectations up front ("it's a numbers game"), show funnel stats |
| Bad contact data | One region first, verify every address before sending |
| Gmail permission review | Google reviews apps that send email. Test with up to 100 users while waiting, start the review early |
| Canadian anti-spam rules (CASL) | Check before launch. One-to-one job requests differ from marketing blasts. Not legal advice |
| Students blame the app | Clear expectations, honest stats, no promises of a placement |
