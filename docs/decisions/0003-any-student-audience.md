# 0003 – Any student, not only Canadian engineers

Status: accepted

## Decision

Workterm is for any student trying to land a co-op, in any program and at any school. The home page leads with resume tools and job search, not with "firms that don't post jobs".

## Why

- A wider audience gives more reach for TikTok, Reddit and campus ambassadors.
- Every co-op student has the same problem: many applications, few replies.
- Resume rating and tailored resumes help students in every program.

## Consequences

- Home page copy and the waitlist form no longer say Canada or engineering.
- Small firms that don't post jobs stay in the search, as one source of roles.
- Canadian law (CASL) still applies while our first users and sending are in Canada. Check each new country before we send emails there.
- Firm data is still one region at first (Toronto, Hamilton, Waterloo).
- Scorecard in `docs/brief.md` was scored for Canadian engineers. Re-score before Gate A.
