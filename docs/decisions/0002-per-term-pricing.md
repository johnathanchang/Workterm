# 0002 – Per-term pricing, not $30 a year

Status: accepted

## Decision

Charge one price per co-op term: **$30 USD** (set by Johnathan on 2026-10-31; earlier idea was $30 to $40). The free plan shows matches and lets students send 5 emails.

## Why

- Students search once per co-op term, 4 to 6 times per degree. A yearly plan does not match how they use it.
- Co-ops pay real wages, so the price is small next to the pay.
- A per-term price is easy to explain: pay when you search, not when you don't.

## Consequences

- We check unit economics at this price in Phase 0 (chapter 12).
- No yearly subscription in v1.
- Stripe is set up for one-time or term-length payments.
