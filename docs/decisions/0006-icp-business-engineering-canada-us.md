# 0006 – First customers: business and engineering students in Canada and the US

Status: accepted (narrows who we sell to first; 0003 still says the product is for any student)

## Decision

Our ICP (ideal customer profile) is 2nd, 3rd and 4th year students, ideally in business or engineering, who want a co-op (probably their first) and have sent many emails and applications with no success. We target Canadian and American schools, for example Harvard, University of Toronto, University of Alberta, McMaster, UC Berkeley and MIT.

v1 is four core features: company matching, resume rating and tailoring, AI email drafts sent from the student's Gmail, and a reply tracker. The skill coach and hiring calendar come in the first update (v1.1). Peer insights move to later. Full plan: `docs/core-features.md`.

## Why

- A clear first customer tells us which clubs, subreddits and video hooks to aim at.
- Business and engineering students search for co-ops and internships the most and get paid real wages, so they can pay.
- Four features can be built and tested well. Seven could not.

## Consequences

- CAN-SPAM (American anti-spam law) applies to emails sent to American companies, as well as CASL in Canada.
- Company data can no longer be only Toronto, Hamilton and Waterloo. Launch cities need to match the target schools (open question in `docs/brief.md`).
- American students mostly say "internship", not "co-op". Copy may need both.
- Growth plan adds business clubs and case competition teams.
- Re-score the scorecard for this ICP before Gate A.
