# Workterm

**For engineering co-op students, Workterm finds local firms that don't post jobs, writes your emails, and tells you what to work on to get hired.**

Starting market: Canadian engineering co-op students who can't find a placement on their school's job board.

## How it works

1. **Set up (2 min)** – upload your resume, pick your school, program, co-op term and city.
2. **Find firms** – small and mid-sized local engineering firms that don't post co-op jobs, each with a match score and a "why it fits you" line.
3. **Send emails** – AI writes a personal email for each firm. Sent from your own Gmail, paced, with automatic follow-ups.
4. **Track replies** – every reply in one place, with AI-drafted responses.
5. **What to work on** – compares your resume to what your target firms do and suggests what to build or learn next.
